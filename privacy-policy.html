Privacy Policy for Richmond Harvest Map

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Richmond Harvest Map ("the App") is developed and maintained by Norah Hazelgrove. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Location Data. If you choose to use the "Near Me" feature, the App requests access to your device's location in order to calculate distances to nearby farmers markets and sort results accordingly. Location data is used only on your device to perform this calculation and is not transmitted to, stored on, or shared with any external server.

Market and Event Data. The App displays publicly available information about farmers markets and community events in the Richmond, Virginia area (names, addresses, schedules, and similar details). This data is not linked to individual users.

No Account Required. The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other personal identifiers to use the App.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not use advertising, does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share personal data with third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App displays map tiles provided by OpenStreetMap and CARTO to render the interactive map. Loading map tiles may cause your device to make network requests to these services, subject to their own privacy practices:

  • OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy
  • CARTO Privacy Policy

Data Retention

Since the App does not collect or store personal data on any server, there is no user data retained by us beyond your device's local session.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same location with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact: support@richmondharvestmap.com
